# Code of Conduct

SkillBox is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and perspectives. We want participation to be safe, constructive, and focused on learning from real AI-agent workflows.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without gatekeeping.
- Give credit for ideas, testing, documentation, and code.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's data, credentials, prompts, or private procedures.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading claims.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring contributors to reveal proprietary data or security details publicly.
- Using project spaces primarily for spam, promotion, or unrelated recruitment.

## Scope

This policy applies to Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, reviews, community calls, and other spaces where someone represents the SkillBox project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the [ArturSaleev GitHub profile](https://github.com/ArturSaleev). Do not open a public Issue containing private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, or permanently remove a participant. Decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and prioritize community safety.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
